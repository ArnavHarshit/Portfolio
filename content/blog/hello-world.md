Every engineer has a moment where a tool stops being magic. For me it was writing a B+ tree by hand while building **MiniDB**. Suddenly "the database uses an index" became something I could *see*.

## Why rebuild?

- Reading a paper tells you **what** works.
- Implementing it tells you **why**, and what breaks when it doesn't.
- Debugging it at 2 a.m. tells you what the paper left out.

That's why my side projects are a Raft key-value store, a SQL engine and a federated learning pipeline. Each one was an excuse to learn something properly.
